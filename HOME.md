# ✦ CareerOS

Your permanent starting point. Bookmark this page and return whenever you need your workspace.

### [Open CareerOS →](http://127.0.0.1:3000/)

| Your workspace | Open |
| --- | --- |
| Recommended jobs, adjacent paths, and wildcards | [Discover opportunities](http://127.0.0.1:3000/discover) |
| Saved opportunities | [Saved jobs](http://127.0.0.1:3000/saved) |
| Applications, notes, and recruiting progress | [Application tracker](http://127.0.0.1:3000/applications) |
| Original cover letters and tailored working copies | [Cover-letter library](http://127.0.0.1:3000/letters) |
| Résumé versions and matching preferences | [Profile & Résumé](http://127.0.0.1:3000/profile) |

---

**If CareerOS is closed:** on your Mac, open your home folder → `careeros` → double-click **Start CareerOS.command**. Keep its window open, then click **Open CareerOS** above.

**Refresh:** use your browser’s regular Reload button. The desktop launcher automatically restarts the backend after server-code edits.

This home page is available on GitHub independently of the app. Workspace links open the existing CareerOS server on the computer you’re using, so use them on your Mac while CareerOS is running. The full app and private documents have not been moved to a cloud host.
